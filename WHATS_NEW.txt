v1.1.0 · Stricter ranking
- Grades against your own best photos: drop 3 to 6 portfolio frames in the references folder (sidebar › open folder) and Claude holds every frame to that bar
- Tier comes first: a HERO always ranks above a KEEPER, no matter how colorful the KEEPER is. The slider now only breaks ties inside a tier

- HERO now needs a 4+ moment, a 4+ technical and a 4+ composition, with a sharp face. A weak moment or soft focus drags the whole score down
- Claude checks the face, the puck or ball, and anything blocking the subject before scoring
- New finals: the top 16 go to Claude side by side and come back in posting order inside each tier (🏆 #1, #2...). One call per game, a few cents
- Fixed: on a fresh install with OpenCV 5, the local look score failed on every photo
- Your old grades get redone once on the first rank after updating, since the rubric changed
